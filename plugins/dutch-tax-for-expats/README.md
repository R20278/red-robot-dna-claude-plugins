# Dutch tax for expats & freelancers

A Claude plugin that explains Dutch taxes in plain language for people who moved to
the Netherlands — and for those who freelance here.

## Install in Claude Code

```
/plugin install dutch-tax-for-expats --marketplace R20278/red-robot-dna-claude-plugins
```

## What it covers

**dutch-expat-tax**
- The 30% ruling (now called the *expatregeling*): conditions, salary norms, the
  60-month limit, and the drop from 30% to 27% on 1 January 2027 (with the
  transitional rule for rulings that started before 2024)
- The end of *partial non-resident taxpayer* status after 2026, and what that
  means for savings, investments and property abroad
- Box 3 (tax on savings and investments): rates, allowance, the counter-evidence rule
- The migration year (M-form), tax residency and tax treaties

**dutch-freelancer-tax**
- Registering with the KVK, VAT (btw), reverse charge and the small-business scheme (KOR)
- Entrepreneur deductions, the 1,225-hour criterion and the SME profit exemption
- Provisional assessments, filing deadlines and the false self-employment (DBA) rules

## Good to know

- Figures were checked in **November 2026** and cover tax years 2026 and 2027.
  They are reviewed at least once a year, after the Dutch tax plan (Prinsjesdag,
  September) and again in January.
- This plugin gives **general information, not personal tax advice**. Always check
  the current figures on [belastingdienst.nl](https://www.belastingdienst.nl).
- Residence-permit and visa salary requirements are immigration rules (IND), not tax rules.
